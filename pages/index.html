The workspace

A serious coding surface, made for your phone.

Keep the agent close without giving up control. AndCode brings the parts of an agent workflow you actually need into a touch-first Android UI.

01

Local agents

Run OpenCode, Claude Code, and Antigravity on-device through a managed Linux runtime. Official CLI executed locally · separate account or provider access required · third-party terms apply.

02

Remote OpenCode

Connect to an OpenCode server on your PC, Mac, or Linux machine over your network.

03

Repository workspace

Browse folders, inspect files, and move through projects with a real workspace model.

04

Git review

Stage changes, inspect diffs, commit, and manage branches before you ship.

05

Tool approvals

Review dangerous operations and stay in control of what your agent can do.

06

Scheduled tasks

Run one-time or recurring prompts and keep a visible history of each run.

07

Voice input

Speak into the composer with push-to-talk input and wake word support.

08

Sessions that stay useful

Create, resume, rename, and switch between conversations without losing the thread.

Designed for the loop

Prompt. Review. Keep moving.

AndCode keeps the working state visible: what the agent ran, what changed, and what needs your approval next.

  • Structured tool activity and readable responses
  • Models and runtimes available from one picker
  • Recurring work with a clear run history
